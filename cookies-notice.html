Cookie Notice

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Cookie Notice explains how Alana Lou Productions LLC ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on alanalouproductions.cloud (the "Site").

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use it. Similar technologies (like localStorage, used for our shopping cart) work in a comparable way but aren't technically cookies.

2. Cookies & Similar Technologies We Use

Category Purpose Examples on this Site
Strictly Necessary Required for core site functionality, such as remembering the items in your shopping cart and your cookie consent choice. Cart storage (localStorage), cookie consent preference
Functional Remember choices you've made to improve your experience on return visits. Display preferences, form draft handling
Analytics Help us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it, generally in aggregate/anonymized form. Page-view and usage statistics (if/when an analytics tool is enabled)

3. Your Choices

When you first visit the Site, you'll see a banner allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. Strictly necessary technologies (like the cart storage that lets your selections persist as you browse) are used regardless, since the Site cannot function as a shopping experience without them. You can also control cookies through your browser settings — most browsers let you block or delete cookies, though doing so may affect functionality such as your cart contents.

4. Third-Party Cookies

When you check out, our payment processor, PayPal, may set its own cookies as part of processing your payment securely. These are governed by PayPal's own cookie and privacy policies, not ours. We do not control these third-party cookies.

5. Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings, including viewing, deleting, and blocking cookies from some or all websites. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions specific to your browser.

6. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Cookie Notice from time to time to reflect changes in the technologies we use or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
