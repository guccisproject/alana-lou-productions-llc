About Us

A Las Vegas studio for the pieces you don't want to lose

How this started

Alana Lou Productions LLC started with a simple idea: the photos already sitting in your camera roll are worth more than a scroll past. A photo of your grandmother, your dog, your kid's first birthday, your wedding day — those deserve to be something you can actually hold, hang, or wear.

So that's what we do. We take your photos and turn them into custom digital art, printed decor, apparel, and keepsakes. We photograph the moments you haven't captured yet. And when it's time to celebrate — or remember — someone or something all at once, we make matching pieces in bulk so everyone gets to hold onto the same memory.
